I'm an engineering leader at [Agility Robotics](https://agilityrobotics.com), where I work on the infrastructure that lets a fleet of humanoid robots operate reliably in the real world.

My focus is the intersection of **platform engineering**, **robotics infrastructure**, and **security** — a niche that doesn't have many practitioners, which means most of the problems are genuinely new. We run Kubernetes on robots that walk, manage cloud-to-edge identity systems for devices that go offline, build multi-region AWS architectures for global deployments, and try to give software engineers the same experience working on robot software as they'd have writing a cloud service.

Before Agility, I spent time across cloud infrastructure, platform, and security roles. I care about building systems that are operationally honest — not just technically elegant — and about growing engineering teams that can reason clearly about hard problems.
